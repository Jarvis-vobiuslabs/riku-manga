RIKU

Every night the world tears open. Every night, she stitches it back together.

Read Chapter 1 Learn More

The first rift took her mother. Now she hunts them all. Alone.

Every night at 2 AM, the sky above Tokyo cracks open. From these dimensional rifts, monstrous creatures known as Hollows spill into our world, drawn by an insatiable hunger for life.

Riku, a 17-year-old high schooler, stands as the city's lone, secret defender. After accidentally opening the first rift and losing her mother to its depths, she has dedicated her life to closing them. But her solitary war is about to change. A secret organization, SHINKEN, is closing in, and a mysterious rival who moves like smoke is challenging her at every turn. Deep within the largest, permanent Rift, an ancient evil stirs—and it already knows her name.

CHARACTERS

01

RIKU — The Hunter

17 years old, with crimson hair and piercing golden eyes. For three months, she's fought alone. Her katana, "Akagiri," absorbs the energy of defeated Hollows, and recently, it has begun to glow with an ominous light.

02

KAI — The Rival

Of unknown origin, Kai moves through Tokyo's shadows like smoke. He is the first person to ever match Riku's speed and skill in combat, his motives as mysterious as his past. Is he friend or foe?

03

THE HOLLOW KING

An ancient and powerful threat that emerged from the permanent Rift. Its presence warps reality, and its knowledge is terrifyingly intimate—it knows Riku's name and has made a chilling mention of her long-lost mother.
