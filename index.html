The Story

One rift. One girl. No one else coming.
She's hunted 200 Hollows. Not one has brought her mother back.

Every night at 2 AM, rifts tear open above Tokyo. Hollows pour through. Riku — 17, crimson hair, katana already glowing — is the only one showing up. Three months straight. No backup. And she started it.

Akagiri glows redder with every kill. A secret org called SHINKEN is tracking her. And last week, something massive came through the permanent Rift, looked straight at her — and said her name.

The Cast

Characters

01
The Hunter
RIKU

Seventeen. Crimson hair, golden eyes. Three months of solo hunting since the night she lost her mother to the first rift. Her katana Akagiri absorbs the energy of every defeated Hollow — it started glowing red last week. She doesn't ask for help. She doesn't stop.

02
The Rival
KAI

Unknown origin. No affiliation. He moves through Tokyo like smoke and leaves no trace. He's the first person who has ever matched Riku in combat. His motives are unclear — and that makes him more dangerous than any Hollow she's faced.

03
The Threat
THE HOLLOW KING

Ancient. Massive. It emerged from the permanent Rift and didn't just attack — it stood still and spoke. It knows Riku's name. It mentioned her mother. It is patient in a way that implies it has been waiting for a very long time.
