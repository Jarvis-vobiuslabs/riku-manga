The Story

The first rift took her mother.
Now she hunts them all.

Every night at 2 AM, the sky above Tokyo cracks open. Dimensional rifts tear through the fabric of reality and Hollows — creatures of pure destruction — pour into our world.

Riku, 17, stands as the city's only defender. Three months ago she accidentally opened the first rift and her mother disappeared inside. She has fought alone ever since. Her katana Akagiri has started to glow red. SHINKEN is closing in. And something ancient came through the permanent Rift — and it knows her name.

The Cast

Characters

01
The Hunter
RIKU

Seventeen years old, crimson hair, golden eyes. Three months of solo hunting since the accident. Her katana Akagiri absorbs the energy of defeated monsters — and has started to glow red. She doesn't ask for help. She doesn't stop.

02
The Rival
KAI

Unknown origin. No affiliation. He moves through Tokyo like smoke and leaves no trace. He's the first person who has ever matched Riku in combat. His motives remain unclear — and that makes him more dangerous than any Hollow.

03
The Threat
THE HOLLOW KING

Ancient. Massive. It came through the permanent Rift and it didn't just attack — it stood still and spoke. It knows Riku's name. It mentioned her mother. It is patient in a way that suggests it has been waiting a very long time.
