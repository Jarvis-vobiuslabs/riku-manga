Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RIKU / Vobius Labs Inc. ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit rikurising.com or subscribe to the RIKU manga series.

Information We Collect

When you subscribe, Stripe processes your payment information directly. We receive your email address and subscription status from Stripe. We do not store full payment card details on our servers.

We may collect your email address if you sign up for notifications. We use this solely to send you chapter release updates.

How We Use Your Information

We use your email address to send chapter release notifications and subscription-related communications. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Stripe Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. By subscribing, you agree to Stripe's Privacy Policy. We receive a confirmation of payment and your email — nothing more.

Cookies

This site does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. Basic browser storage may be used to improve your reading experience (e.g., remembering scroll position).

Data Retention

We retain your email address for as long as your subscription is active. You may request deletion at any time by emailing us.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at labs@vobius.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
